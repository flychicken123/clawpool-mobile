# ClawPool App Review Notes (2026-04-14)

## Reply to App Review

Hello App Review,

Thank you for the feedback. Our app does use a third-party AI service, Google Gemini (Google LLC), to provide the AI chat feature.

We have updated the app to fully comply with the requirements:

1. In-app disclosure before data is shared
   Before a user can use the AI chat feature, the app presents a dedicated disclosure screen that clearly explains:
   - what data is sent: user text messages, attached images, and internal user ID
   - who receives it: Google Gemini AI, operated by Google LLC
   - why it is sent: to generate AI responses

2. Explicit user permission
   Users must explicitly check a consent box and tap "Accept & Continue" before any personal data is sent to Google Gemini.

3. Consent enforcement
   If the user does not provide consent, the AI chat feature is disabled and no AI-related personal data is transmitted.

4. Privacy Policy updated
   Our Privacy Policy explains:
   - what personal data we collect
   - how we collect it
   - how it is used
   - what third parties receive it
   - that Google Gemini is the third-party AI service involved

5. User control
   Users can revoke consent at any time from the in-app "AI Data Sharing" screen, which immediately disables AI data sharing.

How to verify in the app:
- Launch the app
- Before using AI chat, the app shows the AI Data Sharing Disclosure screen
- The user must explicitly consent before continuing
- The Privacy Policy is available from the disclosure screen and in the app

We have also added this explanation to the App Review Information section.

Thank you.

## App Review Information

This app includes an AI chat feature powered by Google Gemini (Google LLC).

Before any AI-related personal data is shared, the app presents a dedicated in-app disclosure and requires explicit user consent.

Data sent to Google Gemini for AI response generation:
- user text messages
- attached images/photos
- internal user ID

If the user does not consent, AI chat is disabled and no AI-related data is transmitted.

The app also includes:
- a Privacy Policy describing collected data, uses, and third-party sharing
- an in-app AI Data Sharing settings screen where consent can be revoked at any time
